## The one-liner

**Bud makes fitness participatory and trusted.** It turns "I should work out" into "my buddy and my gym community are expecting me" — with verified coaches, live/group sessions, booking, and payments that are secure, transparent and fully audited.

## The problem

- 1.8 billion adults (31%) globally don't meet recommended physical-activity levels. [WHO, 2024](https://www.who.int/news/item/26-06-2024-nearly-1.8-billion-adults-at-risk-of-disease-from-not-doing-enough-physical-activity)
- Fitness consumers aren't short of content or apps — they're short of **accountability, local access and trust**.
- Gyms and coaches lose members and clients because they coordinate on WhatsApp, spreadsheets and gym front desks with no retention system.
- Health/wellness platforms keep getting **fined and publicly named** for unsupported claims, undisclosed sponsorships and unregulated products. Trust is now a compliance requirement, not a slogan.

## The wedge

Bud's product loop: **join a community → connect with two Buds → plan/book an activity → attend → encourage and repeat.**

The first commercial customers are **gyms, studios and coaches**. They get a branded community, scheduling, member communication, booking, programme delivery and measurable retention. Members get free accountability and discovery. Revenue follows through partner SaaS, completed-booking/programme commissions and a clear Plus membership.

### Compliance as a feature (our regulatory edge)
Bud is designed specifically against the two realities that sink wellness platforms:
- **Wellness vs. medical nutrition therapy:** meal plans are delivered as general-wellness templates, recipes and habit-coaching — never as condition treatment. Prohibited-wording lists, intake + referral triggers, credentialing and editorial review keep us in the safe lane.
- **Gifting & supplements:** every sponsorship/gift is disclosed (material-connection tracking, creator contracts, timestamped disclosure screenshots, campaign IDs), and only PPB-registered products are listed. This makes us audit-ready in Kenya and defensible globally.
- **Money that's actually safe:** every transaction is atomic, idempotent and fully ledgered, with defined refund and dispute paths.

## Revenue logic

Bud does not count transaction value as revenue. It earns KSh 4,000–12,000/month partner SaaS, KSh 1,500/month Coach Pro, a 10% commission on completed bookings/programmes, and eventually KSh 300/month Plus. Conservative plan: **KSh 2.9m (Y1) → 15.0m (Y2) → 46.9m (Y3)**; no three-year break-even assumed — honest for a trust- and operations-heavy marketplace, which is why **disciplined milestone funding is essential**.

## Risk controls
- Direct KSh payments via licensed providers; **no cash-out/transferable virtual currency** without specialist Kenyan advice.
- Wellness coaching stays out of Medical Nutrition Therapy; supplements are PPB-registered only.
- **Every sponsored/gifted promotion is disclosed and audit-ready.**
- **Every transaction is atomic, idempotent, ledgered and reversible/disputable.**
- Paid growth gated on activated-cohort retention and contribution margin.
- Customer/partner settlement money separated from operating cash.
- Expansion only with anchor partners and country-specific legal/payments review.
