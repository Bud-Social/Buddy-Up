## 1. Why this seat exists

BuddyUp operates a fitness platform where the product lines themselves are regulated:
general wellness nutrition vs. Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT), influencer gifting
disclosure under the Kenya Consumer Protection Act, PPB rules on supplement claims,
age-gating for mature content, data protection under the Kenya Data Protection Act
(2019), and payment flows that must never look like customer funds or stored value.

We have done the first research pass (see *BuddyUp Regulated Business Model 2026*).
We need a cofounder whose job is to **turn that research into enforceable product
decisions** — before regulators, app stores or payment providers force the issue.

## 2. Mandate

Own everything that keeps BuddyUp lawful, auditable and defensible in Kenya first,
with an eye to East African Community and global expansion:

1. **Regulatory framework**
   - Maintain and enforce the wellness-vs-MNT boundary across coaches, meal plans,
     programme copy, AI features and marketing.
   - Own the prohibited-wording list, disclaimer templates, scope-of-practice rules
     and escalation SLAs for health-adjacent content.
   - Run the influencer/gifting disclosure regime: material-connection disclosure
     standards, audit trails, creator onboarding attestations.
2. **Data protection**
   - Register BuddyUp Ltd. with the ODPC; own DPIAs for messaging, live video,
     verification documents, location sharing and AI analysis.
   - Own data-subject request handling, retention schedules and breach-notification
     runbooks (jointly with the Security cofounder).
3. **Commercial law**
   - Draft/review partner SaaS agreements, coach booking terms, marketplace seller
     terms, Plus membership T&Cs, privacy policy and acceptable-use policy.
   - Manage the refund/dispute framework with the Finance cofounder so money movement
     is always authorised and documented.
4. **Research**
   - Continuously track CAK, ODPC, PPB, CBK and Consumer Protection developments;
     brief the founding team monthly in writing.
   - Own competitor/jurisdiction research memos that feed strategy decisions.

## 3. Decision rights

- **Veto (with reasons, in writing):** anything that creates unlicensed regulated
  activity (MNT drift, stored-value artifacts, unlicensed payments custody).
- **Approves:** user-facing legal texts, disclosure formats, verification criteria,
  moderation policy baselines.
- **Recommends to board:** jurisdiction expansion, insurance, external counsel spend.

You are expected to operate pragmatically: your success metric is a platform that ships
fast *and* survives scrutiny — not paperwork for its own sake.

## Annex A — Vesting schedule (applies to all founder grants)

1. **Grant:** [●]% of the fully-diluted capital of BuddyUp Ltd. as at [date],
   recorded in the share register.
2. **Cliff:** no shares vest before the 12-month anniversary of the start date.
   On passing the cliff, 25% vests retroactively; the remainder vests monthly
   over the following 36 months.
3. **Leavers:**
   - *Good leaver* (illness, mutual agreement, role eliminated): vested shares retained;
     unvested cancelled or partly accelerated at board discretion.
   - *Bad leaver* (gross misconduct, material breach, resignation without good reason
     within the first 2 years): unvested shares cancel; vested shares may be bought back
     at nominal or fair value per the shareholders' agreement.
4. **Acceleration:** single-trigger acceleration is not offered; double-trigger
   (change of control + termination without cause) accelerates 50% of unvested shares.
5. **Dilution:** future investment rounds dilute all holders pro rata. Founders may
   vote to create an option pool from founder holdings only by unanimous consent.
6. **Lock-in:** founders may not transfer shares other than per the shareholders'
   agreement (pre-emption rights apply).
