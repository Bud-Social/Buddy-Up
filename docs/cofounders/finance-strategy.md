## 1. Why this seat exists

BuddyUp is raising **KSh 50 million for an 18-month runway**, with a conservative model
showing ~KSh 41m needed for 24 months before contingency. Money moves through M-PESA and
card rails to gyms, coaches and creators; artifacts exist only as non-cash loyalty
tokens, deliberately kept away from CBK stored-value territory. The funded plan includes
a payment-operations budget and a board-controlled contingency reserve.

That plan needs an owner: someone who keeps cash truthful, settlements clean, the model
current, and the company fundable.

## 2. Mandate

Own everything financial and the strategy cadence around it:

1. **Controller duties**
   - Set up and own the accounting system (Kenyan GAAP/IFRS-SME as advised), monthly
     close by working day 10, and a single source of truth dashboard: cash, burn,
     revenue by stream (Partner SaaS / commissions / Plus), receivables.
   - Own M-PESA/card reconciliation against platform ledgers with the Security
     cofounder; every shilling authorised, documented and matched.
   - Statutory compliance calendar: KRA filings (VAT/PAYE/corporate), annual returns,
     audit readiness when required.
2. **Refunds, disputes & partner settlement**
   - Administer the refund/dispute buffer per policy (with Legal); run controlled
     partner settlement cycles; ensure the company never holds unauthorised customer funds.
3. **Planning & runway**
   - Maintain the bottom-up 24-month operating model; refresh the cash forecast
     monthly; flag variance >10% to the founders within the same month.
   - Administer the board-controlled contingency release process exactly as written
     in the funding plan — unused contingency stays uncommitted.
4. **Fundraising**
   - Own data-room discipline from day one (cap table, contracts, metrics).
   - Co-build seed materials with the CEO; run investor follow-ups and diligence;
     keep the cap table clean and option-pool maths honest.
5. **Strategy cadence**
   - Run quarterly strategy reviews against the business model doc; keep the KPI tree
     (activated members → paying partners → revenue) current; kill zombie initiatives
     in writing.
