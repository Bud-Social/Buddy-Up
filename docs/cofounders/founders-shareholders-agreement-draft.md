## 2. Board of directors

2.1 Each Founder holds one directorship: a board of five Founder-directors.

2.2 The CEO **chairs the board** and, as chair, holds a **casting vote** on board
resolutions in addition to a deliberate vote, in line with the Companies Act
2015 model articles (clause 3 operationalises the same rule at founder level).

2.3 Board meetings: at least monthly while the Company is pre-revenue; quorum
three directors including the chair or a chair-delegated alternate.

## 3. Decision-making, deadlock and the CEO casting vote

3.1 **Domain authority.** Each Founder decides within their own mandate (per the
role schedules) and holds the vetoes stated there. Domain decisions do not
require board or Founder approval unless they trigger a veto or a Reserved Matter.

3.2 **Deadlock procedure.** For cross-domain or company-level decisions that
deadlock after genuine discussion:

&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) each position is written down with reasons and evidence (one page maximum);

&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) a 48-hour cool-off follows;

&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) if the deadlock persists, the **CEO casts the deciding vote with
written reasons within five working days**;

&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) the decision is recorded in writing and all Founders
**disagree and commit** — no relitigation of a settled decision; and

&nbsp;&nbsp;(e) the CEO is **recused** from casting on any decision that directly
affects the CEO's own equity, role or removal.

3.3 **Reserved Matters.** The following are never settled by the casting vote
alone and require **unanimous Founder consent** (with any board/shareholder
resolutions the advocate requires):

&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) sale, merger, amalgamation or dissolution of the Company, or transfer
of the whole or a substantial part of its undertaking;

&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) adding or removing a Founder (grant of founder equity, leaver
determination, or termination of a Founder's engagement);

&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) any change to the equity split, share classes, or the 20% reserve
and option-pool top-ups beyond 20%;

&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) removal of the CEO;

&nbsp;&nbsp;(e) any amendment to this clause 3 or to the Reserved Matters list; and

&nbsp;&nbsp;(f) [advocate to consider: incurrence of debt above [●]; related-party
transactions; change of auditors].

3.4 **CEO authority.** Subject to 3.3, the CEO runs the Company day-to-day and
implements board and Founder decisions.

3.5 **Removal of the CEO.** By resolution of a majority of the non-CEO Founder
directors, after written notice and a board meeting at which the CEO has the
right to be heard. Removal under this clause is not by itself a bad-leaver event.

## 4. Vesting, leavers and buy-backs

4.1 All founder equity vests **monthly over four years with a one-year cliff**
(25% retroactive at the cliff, then 1/36 monthly).

4.2 **Good leaver** — by default, every departure that is not a bad leaver is a
good leaver, including death or serious illness, mutual agreement, role
eliminated, or resignation for good reason (material reduction in agreed
role/commitment, unpaid equity terms, or a change the Founder did not consent
to). Treatment: vested shares retained; unvested cancelled or partly accelerated
at board discretion.

4.3 **Bad leaver** — only the following, determined by a majority of the
non-affected Founders (or the board where independent):

&nbsp;&nbsp;(a) gross misconduct or fraud;
&nbsp;&nbsp;(b) conviction of an offence involving dishonesty;
&nbsp;&nbsp;(c) material breach of this agreement (including concealment of a known,
material interest declared under clause 7, or breach of confidentiality/IP assignment);
&nbsp;&nbsp;(d) resignation without good reason within 24 months after the cliff;
&nbsp;&nbsp;(e) persistent failure to meet the agreed commitment after written
warning and a 30-day cure period; or
&nbsp;&nbsp;(f) conduct that seriously damages the Company's reputation or
regulatory standing.

Treatment: unvested shares cancel; vested shares are **bought back at fair market
value** — determined by an independent valuator jointly appointed by the Company
and the leaver, or, if an arm's-length financing or valuation occurred within the
preceding 12 months, at that round's price per share. Proceeds payable within 60
days of the valuation. Nothing outside this list is bad-leaver conduct.

4.4 **Acceleration.** No single-trigger acceleration. **Double-trigger** (change
of control plus termination without cause, or resignation for good reason
following a change of control) accelerates **100% of unvested shares**. A change
of control alone accelerates nothing.

4.5 **Death or permanent incapacity.** Treat as a good leaver; the buy-back
mechanism in 4.3 applies to vested shares at fair market value, payable to the
estate or the Founder as appropriate. [Advocate to advise on keyman insurance.]

or at freely negotiated arm's-length value, per the procedure the advocate

## 7. Declaration of interests

7.1 Each Founder completes a written declaration of interests on execution
(current employment, businesses, directorships, significant investments and
other commitments) and refreshes it on material change and at least every six
months; the Company maintains a register.

7.2 Outside work is permitted and is not, by itself, a conflict. Disclosure is
required where an interest could materially impair impartiality, divert Company
work elsewhere, or place the Founder opposite the Company. Conflicts that arise
in a specific matter are managed case by case (recusal, information barrier,
re-scoping).

7.3 Good-faith disclosure is always safe; only concealment of a known, material
interest is a breach (and a bad-leaver trigger under 4.3(c)).

## 8. IP, confidentiality and commitment

8.1 All work product created in the course of the engagement assigns to the
Company from creation; confidentiality is indefinite and binds from signature.

8.2 Founder engagements are part-time (minimum 10 hours per week) unless
amended by mutual written agreement; commitment is measured by outcomes per the
role schedules, with level reviewed after the foundations period.

8.3 The first 90 days from each Founder's start date remain a mutual foundations
period per the heads of terms.

## 9. Director protections

9.1 Before any board appointment, the Company binds **D&O insurance** or executes
a written indemnity to the fullest extent permitted by the Companies Act 2015,
covering regulatory exposure arising from payments, health-adjacent content and
personal-data processing.

9.2 The Company maintains that cover while any Founder is a director.

## 10. Costs of this agreement

10.1 The advocate jointly retained for this agreement is instructed by the
**Company**; the fees are an **operational expense of the Company**, with the
engagement letter (scope, estimate, any cap) confirmed in writing and shared
with all Founders before instruction.

10.2 Any separate personal-review advocate a Founder engages is at that
Founder's own cost.

## 11. Dispute resolution

jointly appointed within 30 days, mediated within 60 days, costs shared equally.
