## 1. Why this seat exists

The platform is built: web + Flutter apps, communities, buddy matching, coach bookings,
marketplace, M-PESA/card payments, moderation and verification flows. What BuddyUp does
not have yet is demand — paying gyms, studios and coaches in Nairobi, and activated
members inside their communities.

Our model is partner-led density: a gym community supplies a real-world cluster, which
solves cold-start better than any ad spend. The funded plan commits to recruiting
**10 design partners and converting 30 paying partners within 18 months**, plus member
activation measured on participation — not downloads.

## 2. Mandate

Own the entire revenue engine:

1. **Design partners (first 90 days)**
   - Recruit and onboard the first 10 gyms/studios/coaches as free design partners.
   - Run structured onboarding so each partner launches a branded community with at
     least one scheduled activity per week within two weeks of signup.
2. **Paying partners**
   - Convert design partners to paid Partner SaaS plans; own pricing feedback loops
     with the Finance cofounder.
   - Build and run the sales playbook: outreach, demos, objections, close paperwork
     (with the Legal cofounder), handoff to partner success.
3. **Member activation**
   - Own referral, ambassador and partner-launch campaigns; report on *activated
     members* (joined ≥1 planned activity with a Bud), never vanity metrics.
4. **Brand & content**
   - Own positioning, website messaging, social presence and local-community marketing.
   - Enforce gifting/disclosure rules from the Legal framework in every campaign —
     no undisclosed material connections, ever.
5. **Voice of market**
   - Monthly written market brief to the founding team: what partners and members
     actually say, what they refuse to pay for, what competitors (Strava, ClassPass,
     Trainerize, WhatsApp groups) are doing locally.

## 3. Decision rights

- **Owns:** sales pipeline, partner pricing experiments within board-approved bounds,

- **Joint sign-off (with Finance):** non-standard discounting, annual contract terms.
- **Joint sign-off (with Legal):** any public claim touching health outcomes,
  testimonials or before/after imagery.

## 6. First 90 days — success looks like

1. 10 signed design partners live on the platform, each with an active weekly activity.
2. First 3 conversion conversations opened (not closed — opened).
3. Referral loop v1 running inside at least 3 partner communities.
4. Sales playbook v1 documented well enough that a new hire could run demo #11.
5. Monthly market brief #1 delivered.

If after 90 days fewer than 5 design partners are live, we take that seriously as a
signal — about the market, the product or the fit — and address it together honestly.

- Confidentiality applies indefinitely and starts now, including pre-signature talks.
- Existing side engagements must be declared; competing fitness-platform involvement
  during the engagement requires board consent.

## Annex A — Vesting schedule

*(Identical to the annex in every founder agreement; reproduced here for completeness.)*

1. Grant of 19% of the fully-diluted capital of BuddyUp Ltd. as at the start date,
   recorded in the share register.
2. 12-month cliff (25% retroactive), then monthly vesting over 36 months.

   unvested shares cancel on departure.
4. No single-trigger acceleration; double-trigger = 50% acceleration of unvested shares.

6. Founder share transfers restricted by pre-emption rights in the shareholders' agreement.
